01 — About

Poetry
Collection.

Bonjour, je m’appelle Enoch Lathif. Je suis en classe de terminale et j’ai tout simplement une passion pour la littérature.

En réalité, j’ai commencé à écrire un peu par hasard. Je me suis simplement lancé pour m’amuser, puis j’ai découvert que j’aimais vraiment ça. Il est vrai que si l’on n’ose pas essayer de nouvelles choses, on ne saura jamais ce qui nous plaît vraiment.

Et encore aujourd’hui, je publie généralement une nouvelle œuvre sur mes plateformes numériques au moins une fois par semaine.

02 — Journey

How I found
my words.

2023

A Quiet Beginning

Boarding school

In 2023, my days were mostly spent at the boarding school, memorizing the Qur’an and following a quiet routine. I wasn't writing yet. I didn't know that, a few years later, words would become something I deeply cared about.

2024

Lost in the Crowd

The beginning of high school

Entering high school, I found myself lost. I felt as though I had no particular hobby, talent, or direction. I was simply moving forward, trying to figure out what kind of person I wanted to become.

2025

Discovering Words

The beginning of writing

I started reading little by little—quotes, verses, poems, and novels. Somewhere along the way, I began to wonder what it would feel like to write my own words.

2026 — Present

Begin Again

Writing, erasing, and starting over

I once wrote around eighty poems, but I wasn't happy with how I presented them. The design made me dislike the work itself, so I deleted everything. Now, I'm starting again—one poem at a time. Twelve poems remain, and perhaps this time, I will let them stay.
